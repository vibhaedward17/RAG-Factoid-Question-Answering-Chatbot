Natural Language Processing

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of artificial intelligence that enables computers to process, understand, and generate human language.

Tokenization is the process of dividing text into smaller units called tokens. Tokens may be words, subwords, or characters.

Text classification is an NLP task that assigns predefined categories to text. Sentiment analysis is a common example of text classification.

Named Entity Recognition (NER) identifies entities such as people, organizations, locations, and dates in text.
